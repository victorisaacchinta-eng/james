# Abnormal vibration on a utility centrifugal pump

Synthetic training material, written for the JAMES Workflow Lab. Not an OEM procedure,
not approved by any plant, and not for use on real equipment.

## Candidate causes {#PROC-VIB-0}

These are the causes this synthetic procedure asks the technician to consider when vibration
rises. The readings alone do not establish which one, if any, is present.

- Suction flow restriction (for example a partly blocked suction strainer). Often discussed
  when vibration rises while flow falls.
- Bearing wear. Often discussed when vibration and bearing temperature rise together.
- Coupling misalignment. Often discussed when vibration rises with little change in flow.

## Prerequisites {#PROC-VIB-1}

- **PV-01** Record the demo isolation confirmation for this asset: an authorised person has applied lockout and tagout. This is an attestation in the demo; the Lab cannot observe isolation.
- **PV-02** Record the demo PPE confirmation: gloves and eye protection are on.

## Non-invasive checks {#PROC-VIB-2}

These checks are made from outside the guard with the pump running. No isolation is needed.

- **PV-03** Compare the local flow and suction pressure gauges with the logged flow trend, and note both readings.
- **PV-04** Check the suction strainer differential pressure indicator and note whether it is in the red band.
- **PV-05** Listen at the bearing housing with a stethoscope probe from outside the guard and note the noise type (steady, rumbling, grinding).

## Checks that need isolation {#PROC-VIB-3}

- **PV-06** With the pump isolated, open the suction strainer and inspect the basket for debris.
- **PV-07** With the pump isolated, remove the coupling guard and inspect the coupling for wear and visible misalignment.
- **PV-08** Feel the bearing housing by hand to compare it with the other bearing.

## Evidence capture {#PROC-VIB-4}

- **PV-09** Record what you checked, what you found, and the gauge readings in the job notes.

## Escalation {#PROC-VIB-5}

- **PV-10** Escalate to the senior technician if a check is blocked, if a finding does not match any candidate cause, or if vibration stays above the demo alert band after the checks.

## Closure {#PROC-VIB-6}

- **PV-11** Remove isolation, return the pump to service, and record a vibration reading after ten minutes of running.
